Who this is for

Actuaries, underwriters, claims and pricing specialists, risk managers, compliance officers, data scientists and managers at insurers and reinsurers. You do not need a technical background: Part A builds the technical literacy the rest of the course relies on.

How the course is organised

The source report has two halves. The Practical Guide covers the ethical, regulatory and standards landscape and turns it into a lifecycle checklist. The Educational Handbook explains the technology, its risks and how to validate it, then applies everything to three case studies. This course reorders that material so that each module builds on the previous one.

Part A · Foundations

What GenAI changes for insurance work, how language models function, and how they are adapted to actuarial tasks.

Part B · Risks and rules

The threats specific to GenAI systems, the regulations that apply in the US and abroad, and the standards that translate principles into practice.

Part C · Governance in practice

The four-phase lifecycle, its checklist and documentation, and the techniques used to validate and explain GenAI outputs.

Part D · Case studies

The full checklist applied to three systems with different levels of autonomy, data sensitivity and risk.
