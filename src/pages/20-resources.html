Governance checklist

The report's lifecycle checklist as a working tool. Name the system you are reviewing and tick items as you address them. Ticks stay in this browser so you can come back later, and you can copy the result as text for your files.

How to use it

The checklist does not prescribe actions and does not replace legal or regulatory requirements. It structures your reasoning. Apply it in proportion to the system's risk and intended use, take your role (developer, provider or deployer) into account, and pay particular attention to third-party models. Module 7 explains each phase, and Modules 9 to 11 show it applied in full.

Source

  
This course is based on Model Governance/Ethics for GenAI Models – Practical Guide and Educational Handbook, published by the Society of Actuaries Research Institute in September 2026 and sponsored by its Actuarial Innovation and Technology Strategic Research Program. Its authors are Alexandre Boumezoued, François Hu, Clara Sikorski and Bertille Tierny.

  
Read the full report on the SOA website: soa.org/resources/research-reports/2026/model-gov-ethics-for-gen-ai


  
What this course is, and is not

  
    
    
  • It is an independent educational adaptation. It is not produced, reviewed or endorsed by the Society of Actuaries or the report's authors.
    • 
    
  • Content paraphrases and summarises the report, with section references (§) so you can check the original. Short quotations are marked as such.
    • 
    
  • The purple "Reinsurance lens" boxes, the exercises and their sample data (such as the treaty clause in Module 8 and the risk matrix in Module 7) are the trainer's additions. They are not in the report.
    • 
    
  • Architecture diagrams in the case studies are reconstructed from the report's text, not copied from its figures.
    • 
    
  • Nothing here is legal advice. The report itself stresses that its examples are illustrative and that applicable law must be assessed case by case with counsel.
    • 
  

  
The report reflects regulation and technology as of its writing. Like the report, treat this material as a foundation to build on, not a static rulebook.
